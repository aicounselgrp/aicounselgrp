Clients operating across state lines are running into real compliance
friction as state AI statutes diverge on scope, definitions, and enforcement
mechanism. A few patterns worth flagging to clients early:

- **Definitions of "automated decision system" vary widely**, and a tool that
  triggers obligations in one state may fall outside another's definition
  entirely.
- **Private rights of action are becoming more common**, shifting risk
  calculus away from pure regulatory-enforcement models.
- **Sector-specific carve-outs** (employment, credit, insurance, healthcare)
  are not harmonized, so a single product can face different rules depending
  on the vertical it's sold into.

Members: if you're tracking a state we haven't discussed yet, bring it to the
next meetup — a shared reference is far more useful than each of us
maintaining a private one.
